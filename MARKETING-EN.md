## Why Retirement ChezMoi

Most retirement calculators ask five questions and give you one number. Real life has an RRSP at the
bank, a group RRSP at work, a LIRA from a former employer, a TFSA, a line of credit, CPP that starts at
65 or 70, OAS that gets clawed back, and RRIF minimums that kick in at 72.

Retirement ChezMoi models **all of it**, with the rules that apply to Canadians, and shows you the result
the way a financial planner would: a clear table, honest charts, and the odds that your plan holds up
when markets don't cooperate.

> **Built for Canadians, by a Canadian.**
> RRSP/RRIF · TFSA · LIRA/LIF · CPP/QPP · OAS · federal and provincial tax · pension splitting

## 🏦 All your savings in one place

<img align="right" src="AppStore/screenshots/iphone/01.png" width="260" alt="Savings tab"/>

Enter what each account holds on any date, the way you already track it, or **import your spreadsheet**
(paste from Excel, or a CSV/TSV file) and every past statement comes in at once.

- **Every Canadian account type**: RRSP/RRIF, TFSA, LIRA/LIF, non-registered, cash, and debts.
- **Real growth, measured**: your historical growth rate, fitted like a spreadsheet trendline, with its R².
- **Two views of the future**: where the *plan* takes you, and where your *trend* takes you, side by side

- **Debts that end**: enter a payment and its frequency, and see the month each debt is paid off.
- **By total or by type**, with the plan's projection and a trend band that continues five years past
  retirement.

## 🎯 Retire on your terms

<img align="right" src="AppStore/screenshots/iphone/02.png" width="260" alt="Plan tab"/>

Tell the app *how* you want to decide, and it finds the date:

- **At the age I choose**, down to the month.
- **When my savings reach an amount**, like $1.5 million.
- **When I can have a monthly or yearly income** after tax, until the end of the plan.

Goals can be in today's dollars or in dollars of the retirement date. The date found is used
everywhere: table, charts and success rate.

Single or couple, with one switch. Add employer pensions (with their indexation), part-time or rental
income, and spending that changes with time: more travel at 60, less at 80.

## 🎲 Stress-tested against 500 markets

<img align="right" src="AppStore/screenshots/iphone/05.png" width="260" alt="Success rate"/>

Markets don't deliver their average every year. Retirement ChezMoi replays your plan **500 times**, with
good and bad years in different orders, and tells you:

- **Your success rate**: the share of scenarios where your spending is covered every year.
  *At risk* below 60 %, *Good* from 60 % (if you adjust after bad years), *Excellent* from 80 %.
- **When it fails, how**: the typical age the money runs out, and in the worst 10 %.
- **Why the first years matter**: success after the weakest and strongest starts to retirement.
- **What would improve your odds**: each change replayed on the same markets: retire later, spend
  5 % or 10 % less, spend less after 75, delay CPP and OAS, save more, work part-time, spread RRSP
  withdrawals, a more conservative mix. Each one shows its new odds, the savings left at the end, and an
  (i) that explains it.
- **The spending that reaches 90 %**, in today's dollars.

## 🧮 The Canadian rules, built in

| Rule | What the app does |
|---|---|
| **Income tax** | Federal 2026, Québec 2026 and every other province, indexed with inflation, with the credits that matter in retirement (basic, age, pension, Québec family-income reduction, Ontario surtax). |
| **CPP / QPP** | Your amount at 65, adjusted for starting between 60 and 70 (−0.6 % or +0.7 % per month). |
| **OAS** | Partial pension by years in Canada, +0.6 % per month of delay, +10 % from 75, and the recovery tax. |
| **RRIF & LIF** | RRIF minimums from 72; LIRA converts to a LIF at retirement, with its minimum and maximum. |
| **Pension splitting** | Equalizes eligible pension income between spouses. |
| **Withdrawal order** | By account type or **account by account**, in the order you choose. |
| **Level-tax strategies** | Spread RRSP withdrawals evenly (or one account at a time), or top up taxable income to a target. |
| **Inflation** | On by default at 2.5 %: spending, salaries, pensions and tax brackets all follow. |

> Every important value has an **(i)** that explains it in plain language, in English and in French.

## 🔒 Private by design

Your retirement plan is some of the most personal data you have. Retirement ChezMoi treats it that way.

- **No account. No ads. No tracking.** The app has no servers and collects no data.
- **Encrypted with your password** (AES-GCM, key derived with 600,000 rounds of PBKDF2) before it ever
  leaves the device.
- **Synced only through your own iCloud**, between your iPhone, iPad and Mac.
- **Face ID / Touch ID** to unlock, an automatic lock when you step away, and a hidden preview in the app
  switcher.

> There is no password recovery, by design: not even the developer can read your plan.

## ❓ FAQ

**Is this financial advice?**
No. Retirement ChezMoi is a planning tool. Results are estimates based on the assumptions you enter.
Use it to understand your situation and to have better conversations with a professional.

**Where is my data?**
In an encrypted file in your own iCloud Drive, or only on your device when iCloud is off. Nobody else
can read it, including us.

**I already track my savings in Excel. Do I start over?**
No. Paste your sheet or import a CSV file: dates, accounts and amounts are recognized, and re-importing
updates the same dates. You can export back to Excel at any time.

**Does it work for couples?**
Yes: two people, each with their own accounts, pensions and retirement date, with pension splitting.

**Does it handle Québec?**
Yes: QPP, Québec 2026 tax tables and credits, and a French interface.
